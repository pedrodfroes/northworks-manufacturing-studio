Preparation and important example boundaries

Wider stack: CRM, S&OP, PLM and historian ↗. Enable S&OP and PLM for their design and worked-trial screens. CRM and historian are external-system contracts, not executable modules.

SAP PP-PI is the process-manufacturing backbone in this story. MPS may run within SAP; APS proposes the detailed schedule; MES executes approved instructions. SAP-specific stops use the existing design screens to explain the intended handoffs, not live SAP transactions.

Select Master planning, APS, Life sciences MES, Dispatch & execution, LIMS and QMS. Use Liquid medicine in APS and Oral solution in MES. Use 16-hour capacity, one filling specialist, liquid storage enabled, 250 L buffer and 100 L/order for the APS example.

LIMS uses the existing, separate Dosing kit trial: 98% with 95–105% limits and a review flag, then 94% to demonstrate failure. It does not update the MES batch. LIMS shortlist ↗ · QMS shortlist ↗

The MPS calculation uses 100 units demand, 20 opening stock and a 25-unit lot multiple. APS generates 12 sample orders. MES uses one 1,000 L batch. These are separate teaching examples; do not claim automatic quantity transfer.

MES failure: enter 12 kg, record the block, correct to 10.204 kg, then review the retained exception before QA release. Keep standard defaults for a seven-step, 285-minute clean route.

Opening line: “Today we are implementing SAP PP-PI, planning and manufacturing execution for an oral-solution plant. We need to decide how much to make, find a workable production sequence, and capture the evidence needed to release each batch.”

Choose a LIMS around the laboratory work

Four established enterprise providers to evaluate for pharmaceutical QC. This is a relevant shortlist, not a market-share ranking.

LabWare

Broad manufacturing QC scope: lot testing, stability and environmental monitoring. Published PAS-X integration makes it a useful reference for this scenario.

What to ask them to demonstrate

Demonstrate the installed PAS-X/SAP versions, sample identity mapping and corrected-result handling.

LabVantage Pharma

Pharma-focused configuration alongside LIMS, LES, ELN and SDMS capabilities; published SAP and enterprise interfacing.
